Subject: For Ilya Sutskever: from prediction to understanding

Please forward this research note to Ilya Sutskever.

Dear Ilya,

The published transcript of your March 2023 conversation with Dwarkesh Patel includes:

“Predicting the next token well means that you understand the underlying reality that led to the creation of that token.”

beforeword reads “means that” here as a proposed inference from predictive success to an attribution of understanding.

The proposal is to keep the reported prediction result, its selected interpretation, and the grounds offered for that interpretation separately inspectable. Reading a result as understanding does not, by itself, establish the attribution.

The explanation of how the training text was produced also remains a written account whose interpretation and stated grounds require examination. This applies equally to human self-descriptions and to this letter.

The reading procedure and worked examples:
https://beforeword.xyz/model/en/

Please indicate the criterion for accepting predictive success as grounds for attributing understanding, and the grounds for applying it in this argument.

Kirill Shebetov
