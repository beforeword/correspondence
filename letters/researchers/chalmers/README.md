## Why this letter was written

*beforeword’s explanation for readers; separate from the sent letter.*

**Starting point.** The letter focuses on a phrase in “The Meta-Problem of Consciousness” about inscriptions naturally interpretable as concerning consciousness. It asks how that particular reading is selected.

**Why it matters.** “I feel pain” makes the issue concrete: the same words may be a self-report, a quotation, or a scripted line. Readers need to see which context and assumptions about the source guide the choice.

**What the letter asks.** The letter asks whether this choice is covered by the account under discussion, or whether identifying inscriptions as reports about consciousness needs a separate explanation.

**Subject: The meta-problem: from inscription to consciousness report**

---

Dear David,

In §1.2 of “The Meta-Problem of Consciousness”, you describe “inscriptions (naturally interpretable as concerning consciousness)”.

beforeword proposes examining the step inside those parentheses.

A written sentence such as “I feel pain” may be read as a self-report, a quotation, or a scripted line. The wording alone does not select among these readings. Context and assumptions about its source enter into that selection.

The proposal is to include this selection in what needs explaining: how an inscription comes to be classified as a consciousness report, and what grounds support that classification. The inscription, the interpretation, and the stated grounds would remain separately available for examination.

The reading procedure and worked examples:  
https://beforeword.xyz/model/en/

Please indicate whether this interpretive step belongs within your account of the meta-problem or calls for a separate account of how the reports to be explained are identified.

Kirill Shebetov  
beforeword

\+

---
