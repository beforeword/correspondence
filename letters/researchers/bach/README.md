## Why this letter was written

*beforeword’s explanation for readers; separate from the sent letter.*

**Starting point.** The letter examines a hypothetical learning agent in the CIMC whitepaper, focusing on changes in how it performs tasks and how it is organized. It asks what would support reading those changes as evidence of consciousness.

**Why it matters.** Readers need to see why that interpretation is proposed and what favors it over another explanation of the same described changes.

**What the letter asks.** The letter asks for one alternative explanation and one claim supporting an interpretation in terms of consciousness, followed by an account of how that claim helps distinguish between them. The proposed explanation itself remains writing to be examined.

**Subject: Interpretive validation: one claim, one alternative**

Dear Dr Bach,

In §IV.2 of the CIMC research whitepaper you coauthored (April 2026), the proposed evidence includes “structures interpretable as second-order perception”.

Your hypothetical learning-agent example combines changes in task performance and internal organization, assessed against alternative explanations.

Please indicate one competing explanation for that example and one supporting claim, explaining how the claim would favor the consciousness interpretation.
