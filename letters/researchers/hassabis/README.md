# Letter to Demis Hassabis

**What justifies reading model output as an account of its reasoning?**

**Status:** the letter is shown in the Sent folder. No reply is published.

## Why this letter was written

*beforeword’s explanation for readers; separate from the sent letter.*

**Starting point.** The letter responds to a quoted proposal to generate text that people can read in order to understand a model’s reasoning.

**Why it matters.** For someone reading an AI assessment, it matters why the generated text is treated as an account of the model’s reasoning and what supports that reading.

**What the letter asks.** The letter asks for the criterion behind that step and the reasons for applying it. It proposes examining the generated text, its interpretation, and its justification separately, including the assessment framework’s own wording.

**Subject: For Demis Hassabis: from readable output to an assessment of reasoning**

---

Please forward this research note to Demis Hassabis.

Dear Dr Hassabis,

Your essay of 14 July 2026 on a frontier AI standards body includes “generating human-readable output tokens to understand model reasoning”.

Please indicate the criterion for treating that output as an account of model reasoning during an assessment, and the grounds for applying it.

Your proposal places this practice within broader scientific evaluations. beforeword proposes examining the interpretive step within it: keeping the written output, what it is read as showing, and the grounds offered for accepting that reading separately inspectable.

The framework’s own formulations belong in this examination. The criterion and its written justification remain further writing, subject to the same scrutiny. This applies equally to human self-descriptions and to this letter.

The reading procedure and worked examples:  
https://beforeword.xyz/model/en/

Kirill Shebetov  
beforeword

\+

---
