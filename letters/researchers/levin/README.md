## Why this letter was written

*beforeword’s explanation for readers; separate from the sent letter.*

**Starting point.** The starting point is a description, quoted in the letter, of planarian tissue storing a bioelectrical pattern memory. The letter asks how a specified experimental result supports interpreting the tissue’s state as a stored representation of the anatomy to be rebuilt.

**Why it matters.** Readers of scientific writing need to distinguish what is reported about an experiment, the interpretation proposed, and the account connecting them.

**What the letter asks.** Using Figure 4F in the cited study, the letter asks for one claim supporting that reading and an explanation of its role. That examination also covers the explanation itself and descriptions of measurements and controls.

**Subject: Pattern memory: the grounds for reading Figure 4F**

---

Dear Professor Levin,

In Part Two of “Mind Everywhere” (2026), with David B. Resnik, the section on mental representation includes “a planarian tissue that stores a bioelectrical pattern memory”.

The proposed reading retains your emphasis on experimental utility and your qualification that mental representation need not involve subjective experience.

For the reset reported in Figure 4F of Durant et al. (2017), please indicate one supporting claim and explain how it supports interpreting the bioelectrical state as a stored representation of the anatomy to be rebuilt.

beforeword proposes keeping the written report, the proposed memory attribution, and the grounds offered for that attribution separately inspectable.

A written explanation adds another formulation. Its interpretation and its role as support also enter the examination; calling it a “criterion” or “explanation” leaves that role to be shown.

The same examination includes written descriptions of measurements and controls, human self-descriptions, the wording of beforeword’s own procedure, and this letter—including its terms “memory”, “representation”, “writing” and “reading”.

The reading procedure and worked examples:  
https://beforeword.xyz/model/en/

Kirill Shebetov  
beforeword
