## Why this letter was written

*beforeword’s explanation for readers; separate from the sent letter.*

**Starting point.** The letter concerns the use of written and filmed material to build models of human values. It asks what happens when a particular passage is read as evidence of what a person prefers.

**Why it matters.** Readers should be able to see which words were recorded, what preference was inferred from them, and what supports that inference.

**What the letter asks.** The letter asks for a criterion for that reading and a reason for applying it to the chosen passage. The account of the criterion remains open to examination too.

**Subject: For Stuart Russell: text as evidence of human preferences**

---

Please forward the note below to Professor Stuart Russell.

Dear Professor Russell,

In “Provably Beneficial Artificial Intelligence”, you discuss using “written and filmed information about humans doing things” to build models of human values.

Your discussion already acknowledges that behavior does not always reflect values.

Please indicate a criterion for reading a particular written passage as evidence of a human preference, and the grounds for applying that criterion.

beforeword proposes keeping three elements separately inspectable: the written record, what it is read as showing, and the grounds offered for accepting that reading.

“Human preference” remains part of the writing under examination. The criterion, its written justification, human self-descriptions, and this letter receive the same treatment.

The reading procedure and worked examples:  
https://beforeword.xyz/model/en/
